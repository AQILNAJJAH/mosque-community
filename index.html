Masjid Al-A'la

Masjid Al-A'la

Masjid Al-A'la, located in Jitra, Kubang Pasu, stands as a testament to architectural magnificence and spiritual significance. Inaugurated on the 8th of Rajab in the year 1405 Hijri, corresponding to the 29th of March 1985, this grand mosque is a symbol of community strength and devotion. Its sprawling two-story structure can comfortably accommodate up to 8000 worshippers, making it a focal point for religious gatherings and communal prayers.

The mosque's design is a harmonious blend of traditional Islamic architecture and modern aesthetics. The main building features a majestic dome that crowns the prayer hall, adding a touch of grandeur to the skyline. Adjacent to the main building stands a towering minaret, its elegant silhouette reaching towards the heavens, calling the faithful to prayer.

The mosque's surroundings are as impressive as its architecture. A spacious parking area welcomes visitors, ensuring convenience and accessibility. The landscape is meticulously designed, creating a serene and tranquil environment conducive to contemplation and prayer. Adequate ablution facilities are available, providing comfort to worshippers.

Inside, the mosque is a sight to behold. The minbar, crafted from stone and layers of brown marble, is a masterpiece of craftsmanship, adding to the mosque's splendor. The mihrab, beautifully adorned with layers of gray marble and inscribed with verses from the Quran, serves as a focal point for worshippers, guiding them in their prayers.

Masjid Al-A'la is not just a place of worship; it is a beacon of faith and community spirit. Its architectural beauty, coupled with its spiritual significance, makes it a cherished landmark in Jitra, Kubang Pasu, and a source of pride for the local community.
